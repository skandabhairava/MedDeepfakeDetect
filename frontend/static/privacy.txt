1. SCOPE & NON-COMMERCIAL RESEARCH PURPOSE
This Privacy Policy governs the collection, processing, storage, protection, and deletion of information submitted to the MedVeri platform ("Platform", "we", "us", or "our"). The Platform operates exclusively as a non-commercial scientific testbed for algorithmic experimentation and academic investigation.

This policy applies to pre-authorized study investigators, healthcare providers, clinical researchers, and institutional participants ("Investigator", "User", or "you") accessing this evaluation workbench.

2. MANDATORY PATIENT CONSENT & DE-IDENTIFICATION
User Obligations
Clinicians, researchers, and users submitting imaging data must adhere strictly to applicable patient privacy regulations (including HIPAA, GDPR, and institutional ethical standards):
- Explicit Informed Consent: You represent and warrant that you have obtained all necessary, documented patient informed consents, authorizations, or Institutional Review Board (IRB) ethical clearances to upload imaging data for research and integrity evaluation.
- Mandatory De-Identification: You must strip, scrub, and de-identify all Protected Health Information (PHI) and Personally Identifiable Information (PII)-including patient names, hospital record numbers (MRNs), social IDs, dates of birth, legible clinical tags, and embedded DICOM patient metadata—prior to transmission. You accept sole personal legal responsibility for ensuring no PHI/PII is uploaded.

3. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
We collect only the minimum data necessary to operate, secure, and validate the platform:
- Account Credentials: Username, registered institutional/professional email address, account creation timestamp, and cryptographically hashed passwords.
- Uploaded Medical Scans & Metadata: Knee X-rays and CT slices submitted for tamper and integrity analysis, alongside user-assigned study labels or filenames.
- Analysis Outputs: Machine learning inference outputs, classification probabilities/scores, confidence metrics, severity scores, and Grad-CAM visual explanation heatmaps generated during analysis.
- Operational Logs: Request timestamps, queue processing IDs, processing queue statuses, security logs, and diagnostic error traces used solely for system throughput, platform stability, and security monitoring.
- Consent & Legal Audit Records: When you accept the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service during registration/login, or provide consent prior to submitting an image for analysis, we record the consent timestamp, IP address, and user-agent. These records are collected solely to evidence legal consent, maintain compliance audit trails, and resolve potential legal disputes.

4. HOW DATA IS ENCRYPTED, HANDLED & PROTECTED
We enforce strict cryptographic safeguards so that stored medical data remains completely inaccessible to third parties, hosting infrastructure providers, and platform administrators:
- Encryption at Rest: All stored medical scans, Grad-CAM heatmaps, and protected study metadata (including upload filenames, study labels) are encrypted using authenticated industry-standard encryption. Decryption keys are derived directly from user login credentials. Neither we nor our hosting providers can view or decrypt your uploaded medical data or study metadata, as static master decryption keys and plaintext records are never stored on disk or in the database.
- Encryption in Transit: All communications and data transmitted between client applications/browsers and our backend endpoints are encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
- European Union (EU) Infrastructure & Hosting: Application infrastructure and databases are hosted on servers located within the European Union (EU). Infrastructure providers act solely on our behalf to host and maintain system uptime. Stored patient imaging and study metadata are encrypted and are not accessible in decrypted form to hosting providers or platform administrators.

6. ALLOCATION OF SECURITY LIABILITY & PASSWORD DISCLAIMER
As cryptographic access to stored image records relies on keys derived from your secret password, the confidentiality of your data fundamentally depends on you safeguarding your credentials:
- Database Security & Leak Resilience: In the event of an external database breach or unauthorized database leak, stored image data and study metadata remain encrypted and unreadable without your password-derived decryption key.
- User Liability for Credential Compromise: We explicitly disclaim all liability if your account, password, session token, or data is accessed as a result of weak passwords, credential reuse, phishing, malware on your personal device, or unauthorized sharing of your credentials. You bear sole responsibility for maintaining credential confidentiality.

7. DATA RETENTION, SELF-SERVICE DELETION & ADMINISTRATIVE ERASURE
In accordance with global privacy frameworks (including GDPR, HIPAA, and app store developer standards), users maintain complete self-service control over their data:
- Item Deletion: You may permanently delete individual past analysis entries at any time via your History dashboard. This immediately purges the corresponding database record and ciphertext from active servers.
- Complete Account & Data Erasure: You may permanently delete your account and all associated encrypted medical records, analysis data, and encryption keys at any time directly through your Profile settings (Delete Account / Danger Zone). Platform administrators also retain authority to erase accounts upon request or during study decommissioning. Deletion from active systems is immediate and irreversible.
- Limited Retention Exclusions: Limited consent timestamps, security logs, or legal-compliance audit trails may be retained only where reasonably necessary or required by applicable law, and backup copies expire through the ordinary automated backup-retention cycle.
- User Rights & Consent Withdrawal: Users may request information regarding their data processing, correction, or data erasure, and may withdraw consent at any time by contacting us. Withdrawal of consent does not affect processing lawfully conducted prior to withdrawal, but may prevent continued use of features requiring that consent.

8. NO THIRD-PARTY SALE OR COMMERCIAL EXPLOITATION
We do not sell, rent, license, monetize, trade, or disclose user data, uploaded medical images, or consent metadata to advertisers, commercial aggregators, or third parties for their independent commercial purposes. Data is processed exclusively by infrastructure service providers acting strictly on our behalf under confidentiality obligations to host, secure, and maintain the platform.
